# Refine the SBC20FL guide interface

Used the [SBC catalogue](https://www.sbclinear.co.jp/catalog/pdffile/SBC_catalog.pdf) for the 78.8 mm block length and 56.8 mm steel body. Modeled recessed end caps and approximate rail side grooves. Kept the 30 mm installed height, 63 mm flange width, 53 × 40 mm mounting pattern, and existing rail attachment. Shortened the six spacer instances along the rail to the steel body length. The rail groove section and cap step are approximate and the grease fitting was not modeled.

Updated the purchase reference and sheet 6 Rev C. Verified attached spacer dimensions and 15 sampled poses of the moving rear drive against the fixed bed over 700 mm.
