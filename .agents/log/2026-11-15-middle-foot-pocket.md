# Correct middle carriage foot clearance

Replaced the partial relief in the middle foot with a centered 65 mm open pocket, 6.5 mm deep, with R3 internal corners. The wall is 4 mm from center. The pocket floor fully seats the two near clamp screw heads. The opposite two Ø12 counterbores remain separate. Updated BP-MON-04 to Rev C and reviewed the section and hole callouts at A4.

Inventor sampled 225 combinations of the two bellows stages at 25 mm spacing over the 700 mm rear stroke. Each pose solved and returned no moving-to-stationary module interference above 0.001 cubic mm in the sliding assembly. The pose trials were rolled back. External vessel and gate geometry were outside this check.
