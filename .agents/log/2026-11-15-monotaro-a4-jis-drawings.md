  SBC. The CAD rail measures 20 × 16.5 × 1060 and has 18 holes on 60 pitch,
  20 from each end, with Ø6 bores and Ø9.5 × 8.5 head recesses. The deck and
  spacer mounting patterns align at 175 rail centers and 53 × 40 block
  centers. Monotaro lists L = 79.8, SBC lists L = 78.8. Keep the larger
  envelope and verify length and K1 compatibility with the delivered parts.
- Checked the nominal 6001ZZ and 51101 dimensions against the block seats.
  The 51101 housing opening is Ø18 versus NTN's Ø20 maximum, and the Ø22
  shaft-side spacer exceeds NTN's Ø20 minimum abutment. Inspect collar
  contact diameter, washer orientation, actual fits and tolerances on receipt.

  interface. IMAO lists the Ø12 H8 bore and 22 mm hub length. Confirm how the
  M5 retaining hole will be supplied or machined before cutting the journal.

  visual references and do not certify the purchased or shop stock grades.

  plate R2 rounds and C0.3/C0.4 breaks remain active. Leave bearing fits and
  thrust faces to their own seat and finish requirements.
