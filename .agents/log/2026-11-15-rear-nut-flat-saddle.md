# Replace the rear TR16 nut pin with opposed flats

- The former Ø30.2 pocket cut through the housing because its center is
  15 mm below the foot. The pin bore was active, and the two housing pilots
  also encroached on the neighboring clamp-head recesses.
- Replaced the pocket with a 25.2 mm open saddle, 25 mm axial depth, and R3
  inside cutter corners. Kept the 6 mm axial shoulder and 5 mm keeper.
  Suppressed the dog-point holes in the nut and holder. The former housing
  chamfer referenced the old geometry and was suppressed. Deburr new edges.
- On the Monotaro 44848072 TR16 × 3 round nut, the two external flats are
  nominally 25 mm across, or 2.5 mm off each side for a Ø30 blank. The flat
  faces react torque against the saddle sides. The shoulder and keeper
  react thrust in opposite directions. The rear foot closes the saddle.
- Moved the housing screws and matched pilot centers from Y = 5 to 13 mm
  from the rear foot center. This gives 4.9 mm nominal between the Ø4.2
  pilot and adjacent Ø12 clamp recess in plan. The guide holes at the foot
  ends have 2.25 mm nominal ligament at the Ø5.5 bore. Keep that dimension
  in the machining review when checking the guide fit and edge break.
- Inventor reported healthy active part features and zero solid overlap for
  the checked rear nut, keeper, holder, foot and fastener pairs. Refreshed
  the assembly review IDW and checked all four bellows-stage limit combinations.
- For fabrication, measure the purchased blank and set the assembled axial
  clearance against the keeper. The nominal model uses 25 mm for both the
  nut length and the pocket depth. Specify real M5 and M4 threads for the
  pilots and confirm the keeper screws, housing joint and shoulder for
  reversed thrust before release. Review full-vessel clearance separately.
- The denser Monotaro assembly sweep also passed at 225 combinations, using 25 mm increments per bellows stage over the 700 mm rear stroke. The test checked moving modules against the fixed front and bed.
