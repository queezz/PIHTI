# Organize the Monotaro drive by assembly order

Moved the single TR16 nut to the bed. Moved the rear foot, flange clamp, guides, spacers, saddle, keeper and associated fasteners into a new rear driven carriage subassembly inside the bed. Kept the vacuum flange and attached hardware in the rear module. The flexible bed locates the nut on the screw axis and ties the mechanical carriage to the rear vacuum module.

Checked 15 poses across 700 mm in the sliding assembly. The nut and carriage followed the rear vacuum module and Inventor reported no interference between those moving components and stationary bed components. Added the install sequence in bellows-probe/monotaro-assembly-order.md. This sweep does not include vessel clearance or prove bench installation access.
