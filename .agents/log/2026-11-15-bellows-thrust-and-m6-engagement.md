# Screen the thrust load and M6 engagement

- The modeled bellows has 58–64.7 mm inside-to-outside diameters and carries no supplier part number or spring data. At 101.325 kPa differential, those diameters give a pressure-force estimate of 0.27–0.33 kN per bellows. For two equal bellows in series, the pressure loads cancel at the floating middle and the rear sees one bellows pressure resultant. Actual thrust depends on installed effective area, probe and seal pressure areas, and the bellows force versus stroke.
- The ASH125 grip radius is 50.5 mm. At 50 N tangential hand force the torque is 2.53 N·m. A single-start TR16 × 3 screw with 14.5 mm nominal pitch diameter and assumed thread friction of 0.05–0.20 generates 1.26–2.95 kN against a resisting load, neglecting bearing drag. At 100 N the estimate doubles. The frictionless reference at 50 N is 5.29 kN. Do not add vacuum thrust directly to torque-derived axial force. Vacuum and spring loads affect the torque required to move the carriage, and the screw reacts the resulting load.
- Inventor shows ISO 4762 M6 × 60 screws through a 44 mm support grip and 10 mm table, leaving 6 mm nominal penetration in the backing plate. Entry chamfer and screw tip reduce usable full-thread engagement. Backing plate material is Generic in Inventor.
- A 30 mm shaft height and approximately 10 mm lever from the bolt row to the tipping edge give approximately 1.5 kN per M6 screw per 1 kN axial thrust in a symmetric lifted-joint screening calculation. At 50 N hand force the calculated load is about 1.9–4.4 kN per screw before checking preload, load sharing and prying. The NASA RP-1228 pullout screen uses pi times pitch diameter times engagement times material shear stress divided by three. M6 × 1 with 5–6 mm usable engagement and an illustrative 80–100 MPa shear stress gives about 2.2–3.4 kN per thread before applying a safety factor. The material and shear values are unverified.
- The current stack does not pass this screening across the plausible handwheel range. Leave machining drawings at review status. Establish the actual bellows load and maximum wheel torque, then verify the backing alloy, usable engagement and preload. Positive through-fastening with steel nuts or a qualified steel insert would remove reliance on the shallow aluminum threads.
