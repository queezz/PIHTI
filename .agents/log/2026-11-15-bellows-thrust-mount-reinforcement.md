# Reinforce the lead screw mounts

- Measured 60 × 20 × 50 mm blocks with a 30 mm screw center height, a 10 mm table, and two extrusions at 130 mm center spacing. Each block has two M6 screws in one transverse row at 46 mm spacing.
- Added two 99.5 × 60 × 10 mm backing plates, four M5 flush screws per plate and four M6 × 60 support screws into the backing plates. The M6 nominal engagement is 6 mm before chamfers and thread runout. Shifted the existing 1,000 mm profiles 50 mm toward the wheel. Each support center now lies within the longitudinal extent of both profiles.
- Moved the backing screws 10 mm inward from the first trial. The closest screw center is 17.25 mm from the wheel end of the table. The nominal Ø10.2 mm countersink leaves 12.15 mm to that edge. The backing plate is inset 4.25 mm.
- Screened the load path at 1 kN axial thrust on one block. The assumed modulus is 69 GPa for aluminum. The 30 mm offset produces a 30 N·m overturning moment. A solid rectangular block model gives 0.0033 mm elastic bending at shaft height. It omits the bore, fasteners and contact.
- For the thrust direction, a longitudinal beam strip with a 98 mm effective width and an assumed 50 mm distance to rotational restraint gives 0.080 mm equivalent shaft displacement per kN for the bare 10 mm plate, 0.040 mm for two layers sharing curvature without composite action, and 0.010 mm for full composite action. The backing plate covers only part of the load path, so the latter two values are sensitivity cases. The real displacement depends on plate-to-profile restraint, backing contact and bolt preload. The square-edged base with a 10 mm nominal distance from bolt row to tipping edge implies a 3 kN total bolt-row tension per kN of thrust in a simple tipping equilibrium after lift. Check joint preload, material and usable thread engagement against measured bellows force and maximum drive torque before manufacture.
- Inventor solved all 225 sampled combinations of the two bellows stages on a 25 mm grid across the 700 mm rear stroke. No overlap was reported between the checked moving and stationary modules with the inset screws.
