# JIS80 glass window retainer

Adaptation of the yellow `akf-100-window.ipt` retainer for the four-hole side
of Kuzmin's ICF152 adapter. The photographs are consistent with the JIS
VF/VG80 pattern. Kuzmin confirmed that the existing FDM vessel gasket fits
the adapter groove as supplied.

## Geometry

| Feature | Dimension, mm |
| --- | ---: |
| Outside diameter | 160 |
| Mounting holes | 4 × Ø12 through |
| Bolt circle | Ø135 |
| Glass | Existing Ø115 × 10 |
| Glass recess | Ø115.4 × 10 deep |
| Viewing opening | Ø100 |
| Retaining lip thickness | 3 |
| Overall thickness | 13 |

The mounting pattern follows the JIS80 nominal dimensions in the
[Toste flange catalogue, page 22](https://www.toste.co.jp/wp-content/themes/toste_co/force-download.php?file=catalog_flange.pdf).
It is not the ISO-F100 eight-hole pattern. The photographs establish the
adapter's four-hole form, but are not precision measurements of every feature.
The existing glass, gasket and recess geometry are retained.

## Files

- `jis80-glass-115-retainer.ipt`: independent native Inventor part.
- `jis80-glass-115-retainer.stl`: printable export in millimetres, lip down.
- `jis80-glass-115-retainer.stp`: solid STEP export.
- `jis80-glass-115-window.iam`: retainer and the existing `glass-115-10.ipt`.
  The adapter and gasket are not modelled in this two-part assembly.

The original AKF retainer and assembly remain unchanged. The new assembly
preserves the original glass-to-retainer rigid joint and glass seating.
There is 0.2 mm radial glass clearance, 3.8 mm nominal material between a
bolt bore and the recess, and 6.5 mm between a bolt bore and the outside
edge, before the inherited chamfers.
