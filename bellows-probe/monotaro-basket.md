The stage uses three blocks per rail. Get SBC or MonotaRO to confirm the two loose blocks are compatible with the K1 sets, or quote two factory-matched three-block rails with the hole pattern below. The [MonotaRO set table](https://www.monotaro.com/g/00248818/) gives the block length as 79.8 mm and the [SBC Japan drawing](https://www.sbclinear.co.jp/products/linearail/sbifl.html) gives 78.8 mm. Check the delivered block and grease-fitting envelope. Confirm the split collar's axial capacity and contact with the 51101 shaft washer before releasing the drive.

The machine shop faces the screw stock and cuts the Ø12 journals, wheel flat and end tap. Mill the working round nut to 25 mm across flats and fit the keeper with axial clearance. Drill and tap the ASH125 steel boss M5 × 0.8 midway along its 22 mm bore. The saddle, keeper, supports, backing plates, axial spacer and retaining washer are shop parts.

## Desk plate rail-hole check

Measured the current Inventor plate and both installed rail envelopes. Compared the 36 hole axes directly with the [shop drawing, sheet 04](Bellows-Monotaro-A4-JIS-man.pdf#page=4), the [exact rail set](https://www.monotaro.com/p/3304/0156/) and [SBC's SBI-FL dimensions](https://www.sbclinear.co.jp/products/linearail/sbifl.html).

| Dimension | Verified nominal |
| --- | --- |
| Plate | 1,092 × 215 × 10 mm |
| Rail threads in plate | 36 × M6 × 1 through |
| Rail rows | 175 mm apart, each 20 mm in from its long edge |
| Holes along each rail | 18 on 60 mm pitch, spanning 1,020 mm |
| Rail end to nearest hole | 20 mm at both ends of the 1,060 mm rail |
| Rail ends relative to plate | 27 mm in at the wheel end, 5 mm in at the vessel end |
| Plate coordinates from wheel end | First hole at 47 mm, last at 1,067 mm, on both rows |
| Rail screw seats | Ø6 through, Ø9.5 counterbore × 8.5 mm deep, giving 8 mm nominal thread engagement with M6 × 16 |

The 36 axes match at nominal CAD geometry. Before tapping the plate, lay both delivered rails on the layout and check the 20 mm end offsets, the first and last holes on both rows, and that the actual small-head screws drop freely into the counterbores. Dry-fit the rails with the carriages to check parallelism and free travel.
