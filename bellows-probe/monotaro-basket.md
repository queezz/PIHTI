## Remove if still in the basket

Remove the superseded end-supported guide parts when buying SBC. Set [76060767](https://www.monotaro.com/p/7606/0767/) NB shafts, [04725902](https://www.monotaro.com/p/0472/5902/) SS20 holders and [33032343](https://www.monotaro.com/p/3303/2343/) SC20UU blocks to zero.

The flange clamps are new: 4 sets on drawing No. 17 of the shop set. The bellows, CF flanges and rear vacuum module are existing parts. The required stroke was confirmed on the previous stage iteration. Check the physical flange register and clamp fit before releasing the machining drawings.
