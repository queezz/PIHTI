# Plasma-Flange-2024

The water-cooled CF150 flange with water feeds and the copper cathode box with its soldered copper pipe: all set and working, not easily modifiable, and always part of the plasma source.

## Role

- `cathode-box-on-a-flange.iam` is the main assembly and the fixed base of the
  plasma source.
- The plasma source itself (`Plasma Vessel/Cathode-Anode-Flange`) goes in
  through the CF70 opening on this flange. Everything that gets serviced lives
  on that CF70 side; this flange stays where it is.
- Water feeds on the flange; the copper cooling pipe is soldered to the
  copper cathode box.
